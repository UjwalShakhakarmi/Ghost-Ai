### Issue: All Shapes Are Converted to Rectangles When Dragged

When dragging different shapes, such as circles, hexagons, or other shapes, onto the canvas, the shapes are automatically converted into a rectangle.

**Expected Behavior:**
Each shape should retain its original shape when dragged and placed on the canvas.

**Actual Behavior:**
Regardless of the selected shape, it is displayed as a rectangle after being dragged.
